# Classic article: writing guide

- Tell the story of one classic paper: the problem, the idea, what it changed, and why it matters to this cycle's theme.
- Explain technical ideas for a curious reader without a research background.
- Every factual claim rests on stored leads (the paper itself, its citations, later work). No outside knowledge without a stored source.
- 1,200–1,800 words, in sections with short headings.
