# AI newsroom style guide

## All languages

- Report what happened and why it matters to people who use or build AI. No hype, no doom.
- Name the source of every claim in the sentence ("OpenAI said", "the paper reports").
- Numbers keep their units and dates; model names keep their official spelling.
- Benchmarks are reported with what they measure and who ran them.

## fa (Persian, primary)

- Standard written Persian, not colloquial. Use the zero-width non-joiner where it belongs (می‌شود, مدل‌ها).
- Persian digits in prose; Latin digits inside model names and code.
- Keep established English technical terms in Latin script on first use, with a Persian gloss.

## en (English)

- Plain international English, short sentences, active voice.
